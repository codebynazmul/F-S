## How we used them

We used these tools as assistants for:

- Writing, explaining and debugging code
- Drafting and editing documentation
- Translating and checking English and Bangla text
- Researching NASA data sources and APIs

## Our responsibility

The development team designed the system architecture and did the brainstorming of features, designs and wording.

The team made the decisions and reviewed and tested the AI-assisted work before it went into the project. We are responsible for the final code, data and content. Any mistakes are ours, not the tools'.

The crop recommendations are simple rules written for this project. They are not output generated by any of these AI models. The app does not call any AI service while it runs.
